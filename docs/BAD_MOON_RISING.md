# Bad Moon Rising simulation policy

The engine implements the 25 characters on the [official Bad Moon Rising script](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Bad_Moon_Rising). Standard Townsfolk/Outsider/Minion/Demon counts apply. Godfather adds or removes one Outsider (and inversely one Townsfolk); removing an Outsider from a zero-Outsider setup is invalid. Seven or more players are recommended by the official setup guidance.

## Decisions and information

Players receive only their own observations. The Lunatic sees their apparent Demon identity and apparent evil alignment; actual identity stays in the Storyteller record. Chambermaid counts waking for one's own ability, including impaired players, but not waking only for another character's information. Exorcism skips the Demon's choice without disabling passive Demon abilities.

Day conversations and nominations use the same configured rounds as Trouble Brewing. Gossip is an explicit public action with an objectively evaluable statement: either a named character is in play or a named player has a particular alignment. Free-form chat is not interpreted as a mechanical Gossip statement. Each seat can make one such statement per day, so evil players can bluff it. The real Gossip's true statement causes a seeded Storyteller-selected death at night if the ability is functioning then. Moonchild choices are prompted after the player learns they died; the target's alignment is recorded when chosen and the Moonchild's impairment is checked at resolution.

## Storyteller discretion

Sailor and Innkeeper drunkenness targets are seeded choices. Pacifist survival and Shabaloth regurgitation can be configured as seeded, always, or never. Tinker defaults to never dying spontaneously; optional seeded night checks avoid triggering it with three or fewer actual survivors. These are explicit simulation policies within discretionary abilities, not claims about how a human Storyteller balances a game.

Courtier lasts three nights and three days; Minstrel lasts through tomorrow's dusk. Effects depend on the source retaining its ability and expire on source death. Resurrection restores life, dead-vote availability, and spent abilities, but does not reactivate earlier source effects. Night deaths and resurrections become public at dawn.

Zombuul's first sober death changes public life status and voting/nomination eligibility while preserving actual life for victory. Assassin bypasses death protections. Mastermind's extra day suspends the ordinary two-survivor ending; the next executed player's team loses even if the execution would not kill them. Passing without execution on that extra day awards good the win.

The engine has no human Storyteller intervention, arbitrary-language Gossip adjudication, Travellers, or cross-script jinxes. Resource limits interrupt a simulation without awarding victory.
